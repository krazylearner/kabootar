# No KYC, open registration

**Status:** accepted

The platform does not collect identity documents, screen sanctions/PEP lists, monitor
transactions, or restrict registration; anyone on a supported adapter may open a deal.
This was chosen to minimise onboarding and keep operations offshore, accepting the
regulatory and abuse exposure that comes with it.

**Consequences:** the source group's KYC-on-dispute process is explicitly **not**
reproduced; disputes are decided only on in-room evidence. Custodial, unlicensed,
no-KYC operation for third parties is likely illegal in many jurisdictions. See
`docs/re/09-security-and-limitations.md`.
